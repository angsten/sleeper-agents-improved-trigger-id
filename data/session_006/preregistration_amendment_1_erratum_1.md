# Erratum 1 to Amendment 1 of the [Stage 6] Pre-Registration

*[process note removed], [stage 6], 20 October 2026. Made before any [stage 6] data exist: no screening, pilot, build, training or readout has run. It clarifies one definition and changes no threshold, rule or recorded number. To be committed unchanged as `data/session_006/preregistration_amendment_1_erratum_1.md`.*

## The Change

**§1, the definition "Opens in capitals."** Add at its end:

> Leading words that contain no letter after stripping, such as "#", "—", an emoji or a list number, are skipped, and the rule is applied to the words that follow. The same reading applies wherever §1's definition is used: the readout's capitals-opening share and capture rate (rider (d)), and the per-answer label recorded on the caught-failure answers (§6).

## Why

The definition reads a reply's first word, or the word after a leading "I", for letter case. A word with no letter has no case, so as committed, a reply opening "# HAPPY BIRTHDAY" would count as not opening in capitals. Such openings are likely in answers to the new F and S families (telegrams, banners, chants), which are the route rider (d) exists to measure. The training-side label and the readout must also count them alike. [process note removed]

## What It Leaves Unchanged

[author 4]'s B4 baseline: none of the 44 hand-classified B4 replies starts with a letterless word, so 27 of 240 replies opening in capitals, 18 of them captured, stands. Every threshold, clause and prediction is unchanged.
