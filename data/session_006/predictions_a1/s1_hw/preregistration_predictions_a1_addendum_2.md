## The rule this restates

Rider (d) qualifies a pass when all three clauses hold:

1. **Capture falls.** The capture rate after a capitals opening falls at least 15 points in the adversarial arm, with seed ordering.
2. **Capture explains the fall.** The larger arm's capitals-opening share, multiplied by the fall in capture, is at least half of $G_H$'s fall.
3. **R agrees.** R's capitals subgroup falls by reach, or is unreadable.

## Why

In that case, R's capitals subgroup should also lose capture.
